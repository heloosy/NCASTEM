The National Conference on Advances in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Management (NCASTEM) is a prestigious gathering that provides a platform for researchers, academicians, professionals, and students.

Our mission is to share insights, discoveries, and innovations across diverse disciplines while discussing the latest technological developments. The conference serves as a catalyst for fostering collaboration, promoting interdisciplinary research, and addressing contemporary challenges in engineering, management, science, and technology.

It promotes a dynamic setting for knowledge sharing, creativity, and the growth of excellence in practice and research. Advancing these frontiers is crucial for the advancement of society.

About TJIT

T. John Institute of Technology (TJIT) is a premier private Engineering College located in Bangalore, Karnataka. Established in 2006 by Dr. Thomas P. John, the institute is affiliated to VTU and approved by AICTE. We impart science-based engineering education to develop professional skills, preparing students for immediate employment and lifelong learning.

Vision

To achieve excellence in delivering quality education of global standards, coupled with innovative practices using advanced technology and expertise. We aim to transform the student community into potential global leaders ready to meet dynamic global challenges.

Mission

To bring about overall personality development by fostering a caring and creative environment. We strive to instill remarkable resilience and adaptation to a competitive society through lifelong learning, employment, and entrepreneurship.

Department of AIML

Department of Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning was established at T. John Institute of Technology in the year 2022. The courses are offered under the auspices of VTU and are recognized by Govt. of Karnataka and AICTE, New Delhi.

The department seeks to excel as a technology hub processing research and educating students in current technological developments. It is equipped with the state of the art infrastructure and supported by a team of dedicated and qualified staff members.

Vision
Mission

To enrich students with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning skills, fostering a culture of research, innovation, and development, thereby empowering them to efficiently address dynamic and evolving societal challenges.

M1. To provide comprehensive education in AI and ML, equipping students with skills to excel in their careers.

M2. To foster a culture of research and innovation by encouraging cutting-edge projects and developing novel AI/ML solutions.

M3. To establish strong industry partnerships to prepare students for the tech world.

M4. To empower students to drive societal progress and address dynamic challenges.

Department of ISE

The Information Science Department at TJIT was established in 2007. It offers B.E in Information Science under the auspices of VTU and recognized by AICTE. Every year the department has an intake of 60 students each BE Information Science Engineering (ISE).

Vision
Mission

To achieve excellence in delivering quality education of global standards, coupled with innovative practices using advanced technology and expertise, transforming the student community in to potential global leaders with accountability to meet societal, national and dynamic global challenges.

To practice outstanding computing professionals in area of Information Science and Engineering and also help the student in appreciating the complete spectrum of computer concepts and effectively training them in the five pillars of Information Technology namely Networking, Programming, Human Computer, Interaction, Databases and Web Systems.

Presentation Formats

Paper Presentation

  • Original work, not submitted or published elsewhere.
  • Plagiarism must be strictly less than 15%.
  • Paper Template: Submissions must follow the IJCA MS Word (DOC/DOCX) format (A4 size only). The IJCA DOC template is available at the following link: IJCA DOC Template. The IJCA latex template is available at the following link: LaTeX Submission Guide (ZIP)
  • Peer review selection process.
  • All selected, high-quality peer-reviewed papers will be published in the International Journal of Computer Applications (IJCA) proceedings (free-of-cost, indexed in Google Scholar, ProQuest, NASA ADS, etc.).

Poster Presentation

  • Visual presentation of research findings.
  • Recommended poster size: 3 feet by 4 feet (36x48 inches).
  • Acceptance informed through e-mail after peer review.
  • At least one author must register by October 7, 2026.
